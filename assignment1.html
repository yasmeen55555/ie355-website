About the Company

BetterUse Engineering is a virtual company that focuses on improving everyday products and making them easier and more comfortable to use. The company focuses on products that may be difficult, uncomfortable, or inconvenient for people to use in their daily lives.

Our goal is to identify problems with existing products and develop improved designs and practical solutions that create a better version of the product. By applying Human Factors Engineering (HFE), ergonomics, and human-centered design principles, BetterUse Engineering aims to make products safer, more comfortable, more efficient, and easier for people to use.

Semester Project

Human Factors and Ergonomic Analysis of a Handheld Hair Dryer

For my semester project, I selected a handheld hair dryer as the product for Human Factors Engineering (HFE) and ergonomic analysis.

I selected this product because I have experienced difficulty using a hair dryer while styling my hair. It can be challenging to control and move the hair dryer with one hand while using the other hand to hold, move, and style the hair. The weight of the hair dryer makes this even more difficult. Holding it for an extended period of time can become uncomfortable, cause arm fatigue, and limit the user's ability to move the dryer freely while styling the hair.

This project will analyze the interaction between the user and the hair dryer, with particular attention to the weight of the product, hand grip, wrist and arm position, freedom of movement, user fatigue, and sound level.

The goal of this project is to explore possible ergonomic solutions and design improvements that could reduce the physical effort required to use a handheld hair dryer, reduce the sound level, and make it easier to manage both the hair dryer and the hair during styling.

Project Plan

During the semester, I plan to study the current design of a handheld hair dryer and determine how it can be improved to make it more comfortable and easier to use. I will analyze factors such as the weight of the hair dryer, hand grip, arm and wrist position, movement during use, user fatigue, and sound level.

After identifying the main ergonomic problems, I will explore possible design improvements that could reduce physical effort and make it easier to hold and control the hair dryer while styling the hair. The final goal is to develop an improved ergonomic design that reduces arm and hand fatigue, improves comfort, reduces the sound level, and gives the user greater freedom of movement while styling their hair.
